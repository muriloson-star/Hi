Place the Unit

FAQ

Common Questions

⚠️ Safety Notice: Ozone generators must be operated in unoccupied spaces only. Remove all people, pets, and plants before use. Wait 30 minutes after the cycle completes before re-entering. Not available for sale in California.
How long does an ozone treatment take? +
Treatment time varies by space size and odor severity. Light odors in a medium room (500 sq ft) take 1–2 hours. Severe odors like smoke damage may require 4–8 hours or multiple sessions. Always start with shorter sessions and increase as needed.
Is it safe to use in my home? +
Yes — when used correctly. The key rule is that no people, pets, or plants can be in the space during operation. Ozone reverts to normal oxygen within 30 minutes after the generator shuts off. It's completely safe to re-enter the space after that window.
What's the difference between the O-333, O-555, and O-777? +
The key difference is ozone output and effective coverage area. The O-333 (5,000 mg/hr) is ideal for cars and small rooms up to 2,000 sq ft. The O-555 and O-777 both produce 60,000 mg/hr and are suited for commercial and industrial spaces up to 6,000–8,000+ sq ft.
Do I need to replace any filters? +
No filters required, ever. Enerzen generators use ozone plates that can be periodically wiped clean with a damp cloth. There are no consumables to buy and no ongoing maintenance costs.
Will it damage furniture or electronics? +
At normal treatment levels ozone doesn't damage most household materials. However, avoid running extremely long cycles in spaces with sensitive rubber, certain fabrics, or exposed metal. Follow the usage guidelines for your specific space size.
Why can't I ship to California? +
California Air Resources Board (CARB) regulations prohibit the sale of certain ozone-generating devices in California. This applies to all ozone generators regardless of brand.
