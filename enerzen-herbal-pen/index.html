FAQ

Common Questions

How does the herbal pen work? +
The EnerZen Herbal Pen is a personal aromatherapy inhaler — a small tube containing a cotton wick saturated with a concentrated botanical essential oil blend. When you inhale through the tip, you draw the aroma directly to your olfactory receptors, which send signals to the limbic system (the brain's emotional center) in seconds.
Is it safe? Does it produce any smoke or vapor? +
Completely safe — there is no smoke, vapor, heat, or combustion of any kind. The EnerZen Herbal Pen is a dry-aroma inhaler: you breathe through it exactly like you would a nasal inhaler. Nothing is inhaled into the lungs — the aromatherapy works via your sense of smell, not by inhalation into the respiratory tract.
How long does each pen last? +
Each pen delivers 200+ quality inhales. With typical daily use (4–6 uses per day), most people get 4–8 weeks from a single pen. Once the scent fades noticeably, it's time to replace — simply dispose of the recyclable tube responsibly.
Is it safe around children, pets, or during pregnancy? +
Since the pen uses only aromatherapy (no smoke, vapor, or ingestion), it is generally safe in these contexts. That said, some essential oils are not recommended during pregnancy or near young children or cats — consult your healthcare provider if you have any specific concerns. The Sleep blend contains cedarwood, which should be used with caution around cats.
What's the difference between your blends? +
Each blend uses different botanicals targeted at a specific need: Calm (lavender, chamomile, bergamot) reduces anxiety. Focus (peppermint, rosemary, eucalyptus) improves mental clarity. Sleep (valerian root, lavender, cedarwood) promotes restful sleep. Energy (citrus, ginger, spearmint) uplifts and invigorates. The Full Collection gives you all four for every situation.
