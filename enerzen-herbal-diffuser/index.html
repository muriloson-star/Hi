FAQ

Common Questions

🌿 Aromatherapy Tip: Use 100% pure essential oils diluted in water only. Avoid oil-based perfumes or carrier oils, as they can clog the ultrasonic plate over time.
How long does the mist last on a full tank? +
The 500mL Pro tank runs up to 8 hours on intermittent mist, or about 4 hours on continuous mist. The XL's 1000mL tank roughly doubles that runtime. The compact Mini runs up to 4 hours on its 120mL tank.
Is it safe to use around pets and babies? +
The diffuser itself is safe, but always research which essential oils are pet- and baby-safe before diffusing — some oils like tea tree or eucalyptus aren't recommended around cats or infants. When in doubt, dilute lightly and ventilate the room.
What's the difference between the Mini, Pro, and XL? +
The key difference is tank size and coverage area. The Mini (120mL) is ideal for desks and travel. The Pro (500mL) covers most bedrooms and offices up to 300 sq ft. The XL (1000mL) with dual nozzles covers larger living rooms up to 650 sq ft.
Do I need to use special oils or filters? +
No filters required. Any 100% pure essential oil works — diluted with water in the tank. We recommend wiping the ultrasonic plate with a soft cloth weekly to keep it running smoothly.
How do I clean my diffuser? +
Empty any remaining water after each use, then wipe the tank and ultrasonic disc with a soft, damp cloth. For a deeper clean, run a cycle with water and a few drops of white vinegar, then rinse thoroughly.
